# Attribution

## Aircraft positions: adsb.fi

Positions come from [adsb.fi](https://adsb.fi), whose terms ask that it be cited with a link to its
home page. Every entity's attribution names it. Use is personal and non-commercial, under adsb.fi's
own terms.

While adsb.fi is failing, this integration asks [adsb.lol](https://adsb.lol), whose data is under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). A focus area lying
inside Live Track Aircraft's circle is answered from that circle's last answer, which may also have come
from adsb.lol. An aircraft drawn from such an answer credits adsb.lol.

## The list: EnforcementAircraft (ODbL v1.0)

Contains information from [EnforcementAircraft](https://github.com/StrausbergAutomationWorks/EnforcementAircraft),
which is made available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

## Aircraft icons: ADS-B Radar

The marker icons are drawn by the `saw-livetrack` library, which carries them with their own
attribution. Icons by [ADS-B Radar](https://adsb-radar.com), used with permission; the icons drawn for
StrausbergAutomationWorks were donated to ADS-B Radar and are under its terms. The ring this
integration draws over a detected aircraft is its own.
